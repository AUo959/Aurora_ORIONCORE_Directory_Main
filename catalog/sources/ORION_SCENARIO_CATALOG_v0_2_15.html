SCN-0001 — The Extraction

v0.2.0

Core pattern: Retrieve a person or asset from a controlled space before conditions change.

Roles (slots):

  • Extractor
  • Asset
  • Gatekeeper
  • Insider
  • Pursuer

Objective: Get the asset out intact (optionally without scandal).

Opposition: Access control, pursuit, and misinformation.

Pressure: clock; exposure

Knobs:

KnobDefault / range
Power distributionrare->common
Institution strengthweak->totalizing
Transparencyhidden->public
Mobilitytrapped->fluid
Dominant solverability | tech | bureaucracy
Scarcityabundant->desperate
Norm enforcementloose->taboo

Ethical hook: Do you harm innocents or burn the Insider to succeed?

End states:

  • Clean extraction
  • Extraction with exposure
  • Partial extraction
  • Failure but key intel recovered
  • Swap or negotiated release

Tags: [Access, Secrecy, Survival]

Notes: Reskins cleanly as rescue, data exfiltration, artifact recovery, whistleblower evacuation.
