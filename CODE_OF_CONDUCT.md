# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Aurora welcomes evidence-based review and constructive participation. We are
committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for contributors and
reviewers, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to correction.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Preserve privacy, security, provenance, canon status, and repository
  boundaries when sharing material.
- Label uncertainty and distinguish drafts, recovered material, generated
  output, and canonical sources.
- Accept reasonable moderation and correction intended to keep collaboration
  safe and reviewable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and publication of
security-sensitive material are not acceptable. Knowingly presenting draft or
recovered material as canon, or using repository access to bypass documented
authority and mutation gates, may also lead to moderation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces, as well as when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a public, non-sensitive concern, open an issue and select **Other** as the
work type. For a confidential conduct report, use GitHub private vulnerability
reporting from the repository's **Security** tab and begin the report title
with `Conduct report`. Please do not include confidential details in a public
issue.

The repository owner reviews reports, protects reporter privacy where
practical, and may edit or remove content, close interactions, or restrict
participation in proportion to the behavior. Good-faith reports and appeals
will be considered without retaliation.
