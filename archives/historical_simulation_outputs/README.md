# Historical simulation test outputs

These two hour-aboard directories were generated by pytest in a sandbox on
August 18 and 19, 2026. They are retained as historical evidence, not active
station state or promoted canon. Their original `run_meta.json` records are
preserved verbatim, including the execution context in which they were produced.

The owner requested resolution of the held directories on September 24, 2026.
They were moved from `reports/simulation/` to this explicit archive on September 25
UTC. No simulation was rerun and no provenance fields were rewritten.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) maps all 18 original paths to archive paths and
records byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes. Every hash matches the earlier
[live-review inventory](../../reports/analysis/live_review_20260924/evidence.json).
Earlier reports that mention the original paths remain historical snapshots;
use this manifest to resolve them. No executable consumer naming either dated
directory was found in `tools/` or `tests/` during the archive review.

Archive files must remain byte-preserved. To restore a file, copy its archive
path to its original path from the manifest and verify the recorded SHA-256;
restoration does not authorize runtime execution. The archive is outside the
active generated-report area; no execution-context exemption was added.
